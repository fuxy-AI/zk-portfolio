ABOUT

I’m a 3D Artist and Generalist based in Budapest, working across games, cinematic and real-time projects. I enjoy solving both artistic and technical challenges and building assets that hold up from close-up details to the complete shot.

My workflow covers modeling, sculpting, UVs, texturing, shading, look development, rendering and compositing. I care about clean structure, efficient iteration and visual consistency, while staying flexible enough to adapt the pipeline to each project.
